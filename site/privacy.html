Privacy Policy

EPG OG · Last updated 2 November 2026

EPG OG is a free TV guide app. It is built to work without collecting any personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

  • Watchlist: shows you save are stored only on your phone and are never sent to us.
  • Reminders: show reminders are scheduled by your phone's own notification system. You can turn them off at any time in your phone's settings.

Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Network requests

The app downloads TV listings and channel logos from public web servers (GitHub Pages and the broadcasters' image hosts). Like any website, those servers may briefly log standard technical information such as your IP address. We do not receive or use that information.

Buy Me a Coffee

The Buy Me a Coffee button opens buymeacoffee.com in your browser. Any payment you choose to make is handled entirely by Buy Me a Coffee under its own privacy policy. We never see your payment details.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

For questions, use the developer contact details on the app's Google Play or App Store listing.
